## RAID Review Process

The RAID Log will be reviewed during weekly project status meetings.

### Review Activities

- Review newly identified risks.
- Update existing risk status.
- Track open issues and actions.
- Validate project assumptions.
- Review upcoming dependencies.
- Assign or update owners.
- Escalate high-impact items when required.

### Escalation Rule

Any item with a **High impact** that could materially affect the project timeline, scope, budget or launch readiness should be reviewed by the Project Manager and escalated to the appropriate stakeholder when necessary.
