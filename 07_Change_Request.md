# Project Reboot
## Change Request

### Change Request ID

**CR-001**

### Requested By

Marketing Team

### Date

Day 20 of Recovery Phase

### Change Request

The Marketing Team has requested an additional feature for SmartSpend:

> **Add a personalised monthly spending summary that users can share directly through social media.**

The request was raised after the original project scope had already been prioritised and approved.

---

## Reason for Change

The Marketing Team believes the feature could increase user engagement and create an additional promotional opportunity during the product launch.

---

## Impact Assessment

| Area | Impact | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | High | Adds a new product capability |
| Timeline | Medium–High | Additional design, development and testing required |
| Budget | Medium | May require additional development resources |
| Resources | Medium | Engineering, UX/UI and testing effort required |
| Risk | Medium–High | Could affect the recovery timeline |
| Launch | High | May delay the controlled launch if included in the current release |

---

## Options Considered

### Option 1 — Include in Current Release

**Advantages**
- Feature is available at launch.
- Marketing can use it during launch activities.

**Disadvantages**
- Increases pressure on the recovery timeline.
- May require additional resources.
- Could increase launch risk.

---

### Option 2 — Move Feature to Phase 2

**Advantages**
- Protects the current recovery timeline.
- Allows the core SmartSpend functionality to remain the priority.
- Gives the team more time to design and test the feature.

**Disadvantages**
- Feature will not be available at initial launch.
- Marketing may need to adjust its launch plans.

---

### Option 3 — Reduce the Scope of the Request

Develop a basic monthly spending summary without direct social media sharing and consider social sharing as a future enhancement.

**Advantages**
- Smaller development effort.
- Lower impact on timeline.
- Provides some value to users.

**Disadvantages**
- Does not fully meet the original request.

---

## Project Manager Assessment

The requested feature has a potential impact on the recovery timeline and launch readiness.

Since the project is already operating under a 30-day recovery window, the change should go through formal stakeholder review before being added to the current release scope.

The Project Manager will present the impact assessment to the Product Manager and Senior Management for a decision.

---

## Decision

**Decision Status:** Pending Stakeholder Approval

### Decision Criteria

The final decision should consider:

- Business value
- Customer value
- Timeline impact
- Resource availability
- Budget impact
- Project risk
- Launch priorities

---

## Change Management Process

1. Change request submitted.
2. Business need documented.
3. Scope impact assessed.
4. Timeline impact assessed.
5. Resource impact assessed.
6. Risks reviewed.
7. Stakeholders consulted.
8. Decision documented.
9. Project plan updated if approved.
10. Change communicated to affected teams.
