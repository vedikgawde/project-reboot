## Risk Response Strategy

### Avoid
Remove or reduce activities that create unnecessary project risk.

### Mitigate
Take proactive actions to reduce the probability or impact of a risk.

### Transfer
Assign responsibility for a risk to the appropriate external or specialist team where applicable.

### Accept
Accept the risk when the cost of mitigation is greater than the potential impact and continue monitoring it.

---

## Risk Review Process

Risks will be reviewed during weekly project status meetings.

The Project Manager will:

1. Review existing risks.
2. Identify newly emerging risks.
3. Update probability and impact.
4. Review mitigation actions.
5. Confirm risk owners.
6. Escalate high-impact risks when required.
7. Close risks that are no longer relevant.

### Current Priority Risks

The current priority risks are:

- Timeline delays
- Scope creep
- Limited engineering resources
- Compliance approval delays
- Critical defects during testing
