# Project Reboot
## 30-Day Recovery Timeline

The recovery phase is divided into four stages: **Assess, Align, Recover and Prepare for Launch.**

| Phase | Days | Key Activities | Output |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Assess | 1–5 | Review project status, identify issues, analyse delays | Project Health Assessment |
| Align | 6–10 | Review requirements, prioritise scope, align stakeholders | Approved Priorities |
| Recover | 11–23 | Execute recovery tasks, manage risks, resolve issues | Recovery Progress |
| Launch Ready | 24–30 | Testing, approvals, final checks and launch planning | Launch Readiness |

---

## Phase 1 — Assess
### Days 1–5

**Day 1**
- Project kickoff
- Review current project status
- Collect existing project documentation

**Day 2**
- Review completed and pending tasks
- Identify timeline delays
- Review resource availability

**Day 3**
- Identify active issues
- Identify major project risks
- Review budget pressure

**Day 4**
- Analyse root causes
- Identify dependencies
- Assign initial issue owners

**Day 5**
- Prepare Project Health Assessment
- Present findings to key stakeholders

**Milestone:** Current project situation understood.

---

## Phase 2 — Align
### Days 6–10

**Day 6**
- Review existing product requirements

**Day 7**
- Identify unclear and changing requirements

**Day 8**
- Prioritise requirements using business impact and launch necessity

**Day 9**
- Conduct stakeholder alignment meeting

**Day 10**
- Confirm priorities
- Approve revised scope

**Milestone:** Project scope and priorities aligned.

---

## Phase 3 — Recover
### Days 11–23

**Days 11–13**
- Reassign critical tasks
- Start priority activities
- Track dependencies

**Days 14–16**
- Monitor progress
- Resolve high-priority issues
- Review project risks

**Days 17–19**
- Conduct stakeholder progress review
- Update RAID Log
- Review resource requirements

**Days 20–21**
- Evaluate change requests
- Assess impact on scope and timeline

**Days 22–23**
- Complete critical recovery activities
- Review project health

**Milestone:** Critical project activities back on track.

---

## Phase 4 — Launch Ready
### Days 24–30

**Days 24–25**
- Prepare testing activities
- Confirm testing responsibilities

**Days 26–27**
- Track defects
- Resolve critical issues

**Day 28**
- Compliance and stakeholder review

**Day 29**
- Final launch readiness assessment

**Day 30**
- Management review
- Final launch decision
- Document lessons learned

**Milestone:** Project ready for controlled launch.

---

## Key Milestones

| Day | Milestone |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Project Health Assessment Complete |
| 10 | Scope & Priorities Approved |
| 19 | Recovery Progress Review |
| 23 | Critical Activities Recovered |
| 29 | Launch Readiness Assessment |
| 30 | Management Review & Launch Decision |

---

## Project Monitoring

Project progress will be reviewed using:

- Timeline status
- Task completion
- Risk status
- Issue status
- Resource availability
- Stakeholder actions
- Change requests

A weekly project health review will be conducted throughout the recovery phase.
