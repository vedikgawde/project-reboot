# Project Reboot
## Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

### 1. Project Recovery

#### 1.1 Assess Current Project
- Review current project status
- Review completed and pending work
- Identify timeline delays
- Identify budget concerns
- Identify resource constraints

#### 1.2 Identify Project Issues
- Document current issues
- Identify root causes
- Assess impact on timeline and scope
- Assign issue owners

---

### 2. Scope & Requirements

#### 2.1 Review Requirements
- Review existing requirements
- Identify unclear requirements
- Identify duplicate requirements
- Identify missing requirements

#### 2.2 Prioritise Requirements
- Categorise requirements by priority
- Identify critical launch requirements
- Identify requirements for later phases
- Obtain stakeholder approval

---

### 3. Planning & Scheduling

#### 3.1 Develop Recovery Plan
- Define recovery activities
- Estimate task duration
- Identify dependencies
- Assign task owners

#### 3.2 Create Revised Timeline
- Develop 30-day recovery schedule
- Set milestones
- Identify critical activities
- Establish review checkpoints

---

### 4. Stakeholder Management

#### 4.1 Stakeholder Alignment
- Identify key stakeholders
- Clarify stakeholder responsibilities
- Conduct alignment meeting
- Document decisions

#### 4.2 Communication Management
- Define communication channels
- Establish meeting cadence
- Create project status reporting process
- Track stakeholder actions

---

### 5. Risk & Issue Management

#### 5.1 Risk Management
- Identify project risks
- Assess probability and impact
- Define mitigation actions
- Assign risk owners

#### 5.2 Issue Management
- Track active issues
- Define corrective actions
- Monitor issue resolution
- Escalate critical issues

---

### 6. Change Management

#### 6.1 Change Request
- Capture new requirement
- Assess scope impact
- Assess timeline impact
- Assess resource impact

#### 6.2 Change Decision
- Review change with stakeholders
- Document decision
- Update project plan if approved
- Communicate changes to relevant teams

---

### 7. Testing & Launch Readiness

#### 7.1 Testing Preparation
- Confirm testing requirements
- Assign testing responsibilities
- Track defects
- Monitor testing progress

#### 7.2 Launch Readiness
- Confirm critical requirements completed
- Confirm compliance approval
- Confirm stakeholder sign-off
- Prepare launch checklist

---

### 8. Project Monitoring & Closure

#### 8.1 Project Tracking
- Monitor timeline
- Monitor budget
- Monitor risks and issues
- Track project health

#### 8.2 Project Closure
- Review project outcomes
- Document lessons learned
- Complete project documentation
- Conduct project closure review
